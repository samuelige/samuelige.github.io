# AutoTube privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

AutoTube is a private, single-user automation tool. It is operated by Samuel Ige for his own YouTube channel and is not offered to the public.

## What data AutoTube accesses

When the channel owner authorises AutoTube with Google, it can upload videos, captions and thumbnails to that owner's channel, change the publishing status and schedule of those videos, search public YouTube videos, and read YouTube Analytics reports for that channel (views, watch time, retention, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained and audience countries).

## How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to improve the owner's future videos and publishing times. AutoTube does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone else.

## Storage and security

OAuth tokens and analytics are stored on the owner's own private server. Tokens are never published or shared. The owner can revoke AutoTube's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and stored data is deleted when the tool is retired.

## Third-party services

To produce videos, AutoTube sends scripts and prompts (never Google user data) to AI providers for text, image, video and speech generation.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

AutoTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
