# AutoTube

AutoTube is a private automation tool run by Samuel Ige for his own YouTube channel. It produces original, AI-assisted educational documentary videos and Shorts, uploads them to the owner's channel, schedules their publication and reads that channel's analytics.

AutoTube is not a public service. Only the channel owner signs in, and only to authorise uploads to and analytics for their own channel.

Read the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
