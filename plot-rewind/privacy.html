Plot Rewind

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers "Recap automation", a private tool used only by the owner of the Plot Rewind channel. It is not available to the public, and no one other than the owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel and set their titles, descriptions and publish times.
  • Gmail: permission to send email notifications from the owner's account to the owner. The app does not read, change or delete any email.
  • Facebook Page: permission to publish videos to the owner's own Page.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens from Google and Meta are stored in the owner's own automation server (n8n) and used only to perform the actions above. Videos and their text are created by the owner. No personal data about other people is collected.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Google user data is used only to provide the features above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and from Facebook's Business Integrations settings. Revoking access stops all uploads and emails.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
